## Dot Plot vs Histogram: What's the Difference?

Choosing between a dot plot and a histogram comes down to a fundamental trade-off: do you need to inspect every individual value, or do you need a scalable summary of your entire distribution?

For the comprehensive interactive guide, side-by-side visual analysis, decision guide, and tool comparison, visit our complete [Dot Plot vs Histogram Guide](/dot-plot-vs-histogram/).

### The 30-Second Answer

Use a **dot plot** for small datasets (under 100 points) where every exact value, duplicate count, and individual observation matters. Use a **histogram** for larger continuous datasets where interval bins group numbers to keep the overall distribution shape readable. The fundamental trade-off is precision versus scalability: dot plots preserve every data point, while histograms summarize large volumes of data cleanly.
